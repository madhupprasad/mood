Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 June 2026

mood is a private journal. It is built so that what you write stays with you. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information — on macOS, iOS, and Android.

The short version

mood does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Everything you write lives only on your own device.

What mood stores, and where

  • Your entries — the text you write, the mood rating, any feeling labels, and timestamps — are saved locally on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • This data never leaves your device through mood. There is no cloud sync and no remote backup built into the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes this data from the device (subject to your platform's normal backup behavior, which you control in your system settings).

What mood does not do

  • No account creation or sign-in.
  • No data sent to us or to any third party — because there is no server to send it to.
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Optional features

  • Voice notes (where available): if you choose to dictate an entry, audio is transcribed on your device using the operating system's built-in speech recognition. mood does not upload your audio.
  • Chat / on-device AI (macOS and iOS only): when offered, this runs on the device's built-in on-device model. Your entries are not sent to an external service.

Permissions

mood only requests a permission when a feature you use needs it — for example, the microphone for voice dictation. You can grant or deny these in your device settings at any time.

Children

mood is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
